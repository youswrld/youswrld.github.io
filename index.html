02 / A little context

Different disciplines.
One curious mind.

PeopleBusinessTechnologyYD.

I’m a Global Business student with a marketing concentration at the University of Delaware, and the founder of YFD Consulting.

My path began in neuroscience. Moving into business widened the questions I wanted to ask: how people make decisions, how organizations work, and where technology can make a practical difference.

At YFD, I get close to the business before building the software. Through XOXOA, I work at the intersection of product experience, design, and marketing. Alongside that work, I’m helping bring people across disciplines together around AI.

My Guinean roots and global interests shape how I see the world: good ideas travel, and understanding the people behind them matters.
